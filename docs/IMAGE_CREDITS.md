## Attribution and modifications

For every photograph, credit the creator listed above, link to the original title/source and the license, and identify changes. A suitable credit is: **“[Original photo title] — [creator], [CC license]. Resized, converted to WebP; cropped for display.”** The website's shared photo-credit treatment links to the source and license; this register preserves the full source titles.

All local images were resized proportionally (no upscaling), re-encoded as WebP at quality 84, and stripped of metadata for size. The skyline retains a 2000-pixel width; the other images fit within 1280 × 1280 pixels unless their source is smaller. CSS may crop images differently at different viewport sizes. No subjects were added, removed, or generated.

- **CC BY 2.0:** attribution and license link are required; reuse and adaptation are permitted under the linked terms.
- **CC BY-SA 2.0 (DUMBO):** the DUMBO image and its resized/cropped derivatives remain available under the same license. The share-alike license applies to the photo derivative, not automatically to separate application code.
- **Times Square:** the original Flickr page currently identifies the image as CC BY 2.0, and that is the source/license used here. Its older Commons mirror records CC BY-SA 2.0. The credit links to the original photographer's page to avoid presenting the older mirror as evidence for the current license.
- **Grand Central:** the publishing/credited creator is the Metropolitan Transportation Authority, as identified on its original photo page.

The source credits do not imply endorsement of this website by the photographers, Flickr, Wikimedia, the MTA, or any attraction. Photographs show the city at the time they were taken and are not live views.

A compact record of the actual source title, credited creator, original URL, linked license, and verification date is included in [image-license-records.json](image-license-records.json). Runtime image data is centralized in [src/data/photos.js](../src/data/photos.js).
