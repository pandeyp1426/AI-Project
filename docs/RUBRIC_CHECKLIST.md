## Submission actions for the student

- [ ] Read Home and Why NYC; revise any personal statement that does not sound like you. Do not imply you have already visited New York.
- [ ] Explore: combine search/category/favorites, remove a filter and reset. Explain why two places can match “Central Park.” Complete its review fields in the prompt log.
- [ ] Gallery: open with Enter, use both arrow keys and Escape, check focus return, captions and credits. Try portrait/landscape on your own device and record the actual outcome.
- [ ] Planner: compare art and food choices; try one and seven days; save, change controls, rebuild, reset and load. Explain the distinction between current choices, displayed itinerary and saved preferences. Complete its review fields.
- [ ] Read [filterDestinations.js](../src/utils/filterDestinations.js), [itineraryGenerator.js](../src/utils/itineraryGenerator.js), [planPresentation.js](../src/utils/planPresentation.js) and [useLocalStorage.js](../src/hooks/useLocalStorage.js). Be able to explain one event handler, the deterministic ranking/tie-breaker and storage failure behavior.
- [ ] Finish the reflection's personal reason, component explanation, chosen future improvement and ownership/review record. Use your own observations; do not claim Codex's testing as yours.
- [ ] Where available, check Safari/iOS or Firefox, a screen reader and native print preview. Record device/browser, procedure and result in the test report; leave unavailable checks explicitly untested.
- [ ] Confirm the latest GitHub Actions deployment corresponds to your final commit, open the live link, and submit the repository/live URLs and documents in the format your course requires. Disclose Codex assistance according to the course rules.
